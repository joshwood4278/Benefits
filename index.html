100% P&T and federal student loan discharge

A VA determination of 100% service-connected disability or individual unemployability may qualify a borrower for a federal Total and Permanent Disability (TPD) discharge. It is not automatic forgiveness of every debt: verify the borrower, loan type, and discharge status with Federal Student Aid.

Parent PLUS distinction: the parent is the borrower. A parent's own qualifying TPD status may qualify the parent's federal Parent PLUS loans for discharge. The child's disability alone generally does not discharge the parent's Parent PLUS loan. Private loans are not covered by the federal TPD discharge.

Parents may apply for a Direct PLUS Loan through their student's school; the parent is legally responsible for repayment. Starting July 1, 2026, the general limit is up to $20,000 per academic year and $65,000 total for all parents per student, subject to a limited exception for some continuing students. Review the current rules and compare student aid options before borrowing.
