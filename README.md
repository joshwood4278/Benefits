# Benefits
VA Benefits Navigation

The State Benefits page lets you choose a state and VA disability rating without
completing a profile. Its curated catalog includes rating-based state programs
for all 50 states, with rating thresholds and links to administering agencies.
It is not an exhaustive list of county/local programs or every benefit; confirm
current eligibility and benefit amounts with the linked source. Catalog entries
were reviewed November 5, 2026. The profile also records the selected BRS or
High-3 retirement system. The financial dashboard estimates projected 2027 VA
disability compensation using marital status and dependent ages entered in the
profile. The 2027 rates are projections, not official VA rates, and the estimate
is not an eligibility determination.

## Navigation

The app has one **State benefits** page and one **Federal benefits** page. Both share a Veteran / Dependent-survivor checkbox filter: Veteran shows veteran programs, Dependent shows dependent and survivor programs, and both shows everything. The Federal page also has topic tabs, search and an "only worth exploring" filter driven by your profile.
