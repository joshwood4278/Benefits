General benefits

Common federal and Virginia starting points for veterans and eligible households.

VA disability compensation

Tax-free monthly compensation for qualifying service-connected conditions. A current diagnosis and a connection to service are generally important.

Official eligibility guide →

VA pension

Needs-based benefit for qualifying wartime veterans who meet age or disability and income/net-worth rules. Different from disability compensation.

Official pension eligibility →

Dependent compensation

Veterans receiving disability compensation at 30% or higher may qualify for additional compensation for eligible dependents.

Education & training

Explore education benefits for veterans, spouses, children, and survivors.

Federal programs

Survivors' and Dependents' Educational Assistance (DEA / Chapter 35)

May help eligible spouses and children of a veteran or service member who died, is missing/captured in qualifying circumstances, or has a permanent and total service-connected disability.

Review DEA requirements →

Transferred Post-9/11 GI Bill

Eligible service members may transfer unused education benefits to qualifying family members, subject to Department of Defense rules and service commitments.

Review transfer rules →

Fry Scholarship

May support eligible children or surviving spouses of service members who died in the line of duty after September 10, 2001.

Review Fry Scholarship →

Virginia program

Virginia Military Survivors & Dependents Education Program (VMSDEP)

May provide tuition and mandatory-fee waivers at Virginia public colleges for qualifying spouses and children. Requirements include the qualifying veteran/service member's disability or covered service circumstances, the student's relationship and age, and Virginia domicile rules. Verify current tier and program rules before relying on a match.

Official VMSDEP eligibility and application →

Health & caregiver support

Health-related resources for veterans and the people who support them.

VA health care

Enrollment depends on service history, discharge, priority group, income and other factors. A disability rating can affect priority and costs.

Check VA health care eligibility →

CHAMPVA

Health coverage for certain spouses, dependents, and survivors who meet program rules, often involving a veteran rated permanently and totally disabled or specific survivor circumstances.

Review CHAMPVA rules →

Family caregiver support

Some family caregivers may qualify for training, counseling, respite, and other support. The comprehensive program has detailed veteran and caregiver criteria.

Career, training, and employment resources for veterans and military spouses.

Veteran Readiness & Employment (VR&E)

For eligible veterans and service members with service-connected disabilities that affect their ability to prepare for, obtain, or maintain suitable employment.

Eligible veterans may receive preference in federal hiring. Preference rules depend on service, discharge, disability and other factors.

Housing & financial support

Home loans, housing adaptations, tax relief, and needs-based programs.

VA-backed home loans

Eligible veterans, service members, and some surviving spouses may qualify for VA home loan guaranty benefits.

Explore VA home loans →

Specially Adapted Housing

Certain veterans with qualifying service-connected disabilities may be eligible for grants to adapt a home.

Review housing grants →

Virginia real estate tax exemption

Virginia provides a real estate tax exemption for qualifying veterans with a 100% service-connected, permanent and total disability, with separate rules for eligible surviving spouses.

Review Virginia tax exemption →

VA pension

A needs-based benefit for qualifying wartime veterans and eligible survivors. Income, net worth, age, and disability-related criteria apply.
